## Don't let bed bugs spread

Bed bugs hitch rides on luggage, furniture, and clothing, and a small problem can turn into a whole-house infestation in a matter of weeks. They hide in mattress seams, box springs, headboards, furniture joints, and along baseboards — which is why do-it-yourself sprays usually fail.

## How we treat bed bugs

We inspect every likely hiding spot, treat the affected rooms thoroughly, and follow up to confirm the problem is gone. If you're a landlord or property manager, we can help you stop an infestation before it moves into neighboring units.
