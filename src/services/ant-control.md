## Stop ants at the source

Most ant problems start outside and move in looking for food and water. Spraying the ants you can see doesn't solve the problem — the colony just sends more. We find where they're nesting and how they're getting in, then treat the colony directly.

## Carpenter ants

Carpenter ants are the large black ants you may see indoors in spring. They tunnel through damp or damaged wood to build nests, and over time they can cause real structural damage. If you're seeing large ants or small piles of wood shavings, give us a call.
