## Take your yard back

Mosquitoes and ticks aren't just annoying — they can carry disease to people and pets. Mosquitoes rest in shady, humid spots during the day and breed in any standing water, while ticks wait along tall grass, tree lines, and brushy edges.

## How our yard treatments work

We treat the places mosquitoes and ticks actually live: shrubs, the undersides of leaves, tree lines, fence rows, and shaded areas around your home. We'll also point out standing water and other breeding sites so the treatment lasts longer.
