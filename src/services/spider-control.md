## Fewer spiders, fewer webs

Most spiders come inside following the insects they eat, and they settle into quiet, undisturbed spaces like basements, garages, closets, and crawl spaces. Missouri is also home to the brown recluse, which hides in clutter and storage areas.

## How we handle spiders

We treat inside and out — knocking down webs, treating the places spiders hide, and targeting the cracks and gaps they use to get in. Reducing the other insects around your home helps keep spiders away long-term.
