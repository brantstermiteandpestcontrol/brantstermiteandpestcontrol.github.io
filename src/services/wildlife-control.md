## Get unwanted animals out

Nuisance animals dig under decks and sheds, get into crawl spaces and attics, tear into trash and gardens, and can carry parasites and disease. The longer they stay, the more damage they do.

## Live trapping and long-term prevention

We use humane live traps to capture and remove problem animals, then help you with exclusion — closing off the openings and fixing the attractants that drew them in — so the problem doesn't repeat.
