## Don't risk the stings

Paper wasps, hornets, and yellowjackets build nests under eaves, inside wall voids, in sheds, and even underground. Disturbing a nest can provoke an attack — and for anyone allergic, a single sting can be serious.

## Safe removal and prevention

We locate and remove nests, treat the area so they don't rebuild in the same spot, and apply preventative treatments around the places stinging insects like to build.
