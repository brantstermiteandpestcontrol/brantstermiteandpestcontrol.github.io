## Roaches don't go away on their own

Cockroaches hide in warm, tight spaces near food and water — behind refrigerators and stoves, under sinks, and inside cabinets. One female can produce hundreds of offspring, so a few sightings can quickly become a full infestation.

## How we eliminate cockroaches

We inspect to find where they're hiding and breeding, apply targeted treatments in the areas that matter, and follow up to make sure the population is gone. For apartments and restaurants, we set up ongoing programs that keep roaches from coming back.
