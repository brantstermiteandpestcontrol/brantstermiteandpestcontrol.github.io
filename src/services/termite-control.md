## Protect your biggest investment

Subterranean termites are active all across Missouri, and they feed quietly inside walls, floors, and framing. By the time damage is visible, repairs can be expensive. A professional termite inspection is the fastest way to find out where you stand.

## How we handle termites

We start with a thorough inspection of your foundation, crawl space, basement, and any spots where wood meets soil. If we find activity, we show you what we found, recommend a treatment plan that fits your property, and set you up with ongoing protection so termites don't come back.
