## Safe removal, no guesswork

Most snakes in Missouri are harmless, but nobody wants one in the garage, basement, or crawl space — and a few local species are venomous. Rather than risk a bite trying to handle it yourself, give us a call.

## Keeping snakes away

After removal, we'll look for the entry points and attractants — rodents, woodpiles, tall grass, and gaps under porches or foundations — and tell you what to change so snakes are less likely to return.
