Brant's Termite & Pest Control is a locally owned and operated pest control company based in Warrensburg, Missouri. Owner **Tyler Brant** brings years of hands-on pest control experience to every job, and started the company to give Central Missouri homeowners and businesses the kind of service that's hard to find from the big national chains.

When you call Brant's, you're working directly with the owner. We take the time to inspect properly, explain what we find, and recommend the treatment that actually fits your property — not just whatever's on the truck.

## Our Mission

**Targeting pests. Defending your property.** Our goal is simple: eliminate the pests you have today and keep them from coming back tomorrow, with professional, dependable service from people who live and work in the same communities we serve.

## Residential & Commercial

We protect homes, rentals, and farms, along with apartment complexes, restaurants, offices, warehouses, and city and municipal properties from Warrensburg and Sedalia to Clinton, Warsaw, Richmond, and the Kansas City metro.
