# How to update your website

Your website is **www.brantstermiteandpest.com**. You edit it at **https://app.pagescms.org**. Sign in, change what you need, and click **Save**. The live site updates on its own in about a minute.

You can't break the design from the editor. If something goes wrong, every change is saved in history and can be undone.

---

## Signing in

1. Go to **https://app.pagescms.org**.
2. Sign in with your GitHub account, or with the email link if you were invited by email.
3. Click the website project. The menu on the left is everything you can edit.

## The most common changes

### Change the phone number, hours, or address
**Business Info.** Edit the field and click **Save**. The change shows up everywhere: header, buttons, footer, contact page, and Google's data.

- If you change your hours, also update **Business hours for Google**. The help text under that box shows the format.

### Edit text on a page
**Pages → Home page** (or Residential, Commercial, About). Each box is a section of the page. Change the words and click **Save**.

- **Google title** and **Google description** are what people see in Google search results. Keep the title short (under about 60 characters) and include the service and town.

### Edit a service
**Services →** pick one. You can change the description, the "What's included" list, the warning signs, and the FAQs.

- **Order on the site** controls the order services appear in (1 shows first).
- **Hide this service** takes it off the website without deleting it.

### Add a new service (for example, Flea Control)
**Services → Add entry.** Fill in the name, a one-line summary, an intro, and pick an icon. Save, and it appears in the menu, on the Services page, and in the footer.

### Add a new town
**Service Areas → Add entry.** Enter the town name and county, and write a short **Local intro** about that town. Make each one different, because Google ranks unique pages higher than copies.

- For the map: open Google Maps, right-click the town, and click the numbers at the top to copy them. The first number goes in **Latitude** and the second in **Longitude**.
- **Featured city** creates pages like "Termite Control in [Town], MO." Keep this to your 5–6 most important towns.

### Add a customer review
**Shared sections → Customer reviews → Add item.** Use real reviews from Google, and ask the customer first. The reviews section appears on the home page once you add the first one.

### Change the main photo
**Pages → Home page → Top banner → Photo.** Upload a new photo. Tall (portrait) photos of you on the job work best. Fill in **Photo description** too, for example "Tyler Brant treating a home for termites in Sedalia".

### Quote form emails
Quote requests are emailed to the address set up with Web3Forms. To change which inbox gets them, ask Tyler Banks to set up a new key.

## Tips that bring in more calls

- **Ask every happy customer for a Google review.** It's the single biggest thing for showing up in "pest control near me." Reviews that mention the service and town help most, for example "great termite service in Sedalia."
- **Post on your Google Business Profile weekly**, with a photo from a job and a line like "Mosquito season is here in Warrensburg. Call 660-909-7284."
- **Add real job photos** to your Google profile: your truck, your equipment, you working, before and after shots.
- **Keep your name, phone, and town exactly the same** everywhere (website, Google, Facebook, Yelp, BBB).
